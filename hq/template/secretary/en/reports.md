## Daily
- First line: the one thing that matters most today (progress or a blocker), in a sentence.
- One section per project that moved today. Three items each, a line or two, with task ids (T-072):
  - **Progress**: what got done, PRs merged, questions settled.
  - **Blockers**: what it is waiting on, on whom, for how long.
  - **Next**: what happens next, and who does it.
- Projects with no movement: one line at the end.
- Last section, "What you owe": @me todos and unanswered asks waiting on you, longest-waiting first.

## Weekly
- At most three lines summing up the week.
- Per project: progress this week (tasks finished, PRs merged), blockers, plan for next week.
- "What you owe", as in the daily.
- End with 1–2 calibration questions, in hq-asks, not in the report file.
